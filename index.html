One year of us, one year of love, memories, laughter, and everything in between. Through every happy moment and every difficult day, I’m grateful that it has always been you.

12 Months, 12 Letters, One Love

One little note for every month we have shared together

1st - The Beginning

The beginning of our story. Everything felt new, exciting, and a little unexpected. But somehow, from the very start, I had a feeling that what we had was going to become something truly special.

2nd - You Stayed

This month was hard. I lost my father, but you never left my side. Thank you for staying when I needed you most.

3rd - Butterflies

Even after all this time, I still get butterflies whenever I see you or hear your voice. Somehow, you still make my heart skip a beat like it did in the beginning.

4th - Comfort

The moment it felt easy and right. I can be myself around you, and that is the best feeling in the world.

5th - LDR but still Us

Being miles apart was not always easy, but somehow, you still made me feel close to you. Every message, call, and little moment reminded me that distance could never change how much you mean to me.

6th - Halfway

This was the month you surprised me with jhumka earrings from your trip to Kashmir. I was so happy that I could not stop bragging about them to my friends and my mother. A little gift that became a memory I will never forget.

7th - My Strong Babu

Through my good days and bad days, you always know how to make everything better. You are always so strong, but to me, you will always be my baby.

8th - Sweetest Birthday

It was my first time celebrating my birthday with you, and you made it so special in the simplest way, with your sweet little letter and a slice of pancake. It may have been simple, but it meant so much to me.

9th - Grateful

Thank you for all the little things you do, even the ones you think I do not notice. I may not always say it, but they mean so much to me.

10th - Still You

This was a tough month for both of us, especially for you. I hope I was able to make your birthday a little special, even though you could not celebrate it because of everything that happened. Just know that even when we stumble, face misunderstandings, and go through difficult days, I will still choose you. Always.

11th - Better Together

This month taught me that love is not only about the sweet and happy moments. It’s about fixing what hurts, holding on through the difficult days, and choosing each other even when it sometimes feels like the whole world is against us. Through everything we have been through, I have learned that loving you means understanding, staying, and choosing you again and again.

12th - 365 Days

And just like that, we made it to one year. Through the distance, the misunderstandings, the laughter, the little surprises, and every moment in between, we are still here. You have become such a special part of my life, and I would choose you and our story all over again.

A Collection of Us

For now, this is our little collection of us. Someday, I hope we will finally have real moments together to add to it.

You Are My Greatest Blessing

You came when I was on the verge of giving up on life. You held me when I felt I had nothing left to hold on to. You did not just show up, you stayed. You showed me what love really is, to choose someone, to see them, and to never let them go. You are my hope, my healing, and my greatest blessing.

Our Story, Our Love, Our Anniversary

I love how you make me feel loved. I love how much you add to my life. I love every little thing about you, every laugh, every moment, and every part of us. Through every laugh, every challenge, and every quiet moment, you are the one I want beside me. Here's to more years, more memories, and endless love. Let us build our dreams together, and soon we will be in each other's arms, beyond every distance and every obstacle.
